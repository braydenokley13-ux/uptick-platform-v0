# Uptick Local pilot operator playbook

This is an adult, four-week local membership pilot. Default target: 150 admitted members; absolute cap: 200. Marketing text consent is optional. A membership is not the same as admission to a backed pilot cohort.

## Before admitting a real member

1. Open the canonical operator site at `https://pilot.upticklocal.com/login`. Use your own operator account. Never use a preview deployment for shared pilot data.
2. Confirm the release with the technical owner. Record its Git SHA and migration list in the pilot run. Keep real enrollment and promotional delivery disabled until commissioning is signed off.
3. Resolve the legal entity, business notice address, and exact monitored support/privacy email. The current supplied email spelling is awaiting confirmation. Record approval of the public policies; do not claim legal review unless it occurred.
4. Enroll and test operator MFA, preserve recovery access with the founder, and verify that a revoked provider session stops working. Enable the operator MFA requirement only after the account has a working factor.
5. Open **Market**. Confirm one real Market Cell, its ZIPs, participating locations and travel expectations. Classify real, internal, demo and synthetic records explicitly. Do not relabel old events to make the scorecard look better.
6. Confirm each store with its manager. Write down the exact free item, SKU, size, useful hours, zero required spend, zero member fee, funding party and fulfilling party. Ordinary inventory is not automatically a reserved pilot commitment.
7. Open **Today → Destination readiness, incidents and recovery**. Certify the exact supply. Record the primary manager, direct contact, backup, owner approval, shift briefing, current stock confirmation, staff QR rehearsal and escalation path. Stock evidence must be no more than 72 hours old at release. Do not enter a future date as completed work.
8. Reserve a substitute at the same counter. It needs independent inventory/dependencies, enough units, a named payer and clear cashier instructions. A substitute using the same broken machine is not an independent fallback.
9. If a merchant is paying, open **Programs**. Agree a Growth Program: objective, placement category, four dates, fee once, benefit ceiling, weekly placements, buyer/funder/fulfiller, locations, evaluation and any narrow protection. Link its supplies and approve the version. Organic supply requires no paid Program. A proposal is not an approved obligation.
10. Open **Today → Create a four-week pilot run**. Enter the Market Cell, Monday start, target/cap, operator, support owner, backup, budget and classification. Commit separate supply capacity to all four weeks. The smallest backed week limits admissions.
11. Create an accountable partner distribution commitment: partner/source, channel, planned time, owner and intended population. Give the partner its actual source link. A planned email audience is not measured reach.
12. Complete the physical rehearsal below. Record the launch checklist truthfully. Record recovery liquidity separately from expenses. Record setup labor separately from ongoing work.
13. Only after hosted commissioning, public identity/policies, store agreements, support and physical rehearsal are complete, authorize real enrollment. Move the run to **enrolling**. Adult members verify their phone and choose promotional SMS independently; eligible accounts are admitted only while four-week capacity remains.
14. Review the admitted list, then move to **live**. This freezes the baseline. Pausing and resuming cannot reopen admissions or remove disengaged members from reporting.

## Every morning

1. Open **Today** and choose the real pilot run. Confirm the classification before reading its numbers.
2. Read **Needs action**. Deal first with uncovered member-weeks, expired/stale readiness, unavailable fallback, unresolved fulfillment incidents, delivery uncertainty and failed/stale jobs.
3. Call the manager if stock, hours or staff coverage changed. Restrict or suspend the destination while investigating. Issued commitments remain visible; pausing does not erase them.
4. Check **Members & support**. Read HELP and non-keyword messages. The queue includes contextual grant/recovery references and masked member contact information. Record a useful resolution before closing a support request.
5. Check partner distributions due today. Record actual execution and evidence. If missed, mark it honestly and create a replacement plan. Never silently change a completed source history.
6. Record actual costs and active operating minutes. Keep on-call availability separate from time actively worked.

## When a merchant cannot honor a benefit

1. Get the member's pass/grant reference from the member or protected support queue. Do not ask them to post a private pass publicly.
2. Open an incident against that grant. Choose the actual failure, time, severity and owner. A digital redemption does not prove the member received the item.
3. Contact the manager and update destination readiness. Stop sending additional people into the same unresolved failure.
4. Prefer an approved, available same-counter fallback. If unsuitable, choose an independently backed replacement supply and destination. Verify its hours, staff QR, current readiness, inventory and expiry.
5. Issue a recovery with a named payer and evidence. Retry the same incident if a request is interrupted; do not create another incident merely to obtain a second remedy.
6. The member reuses the original pass for the recovery. Keep the original attempt and its evidence. Never fabricate a successful original redemption to get around a recovery block.
7. Staff completes the remedy and records its redemption. Confirm the actual handoff with the member when needed. Record actual cost in the economic ledger; a payer assignment by itself is not proof of payment.

## When a member needs access

1. Ask them to open **Your Uptick** in the browser where they confirmed membership.
2. If that browser session is lost, use a saved one-time recovery code. The member should save these privately from Preferences before needing them.
3. A fresh requested access text is available only where transactional messaging is configured and the number is deliverable. STOP can block even requested texts at the carrier; never treat START as new marketing consent.
4. If all access methods fail, route the case to the monitored support owner for identity verification and documented recovery. Do not share someone else's private link or claim to have a fully automated identity-recovery process that has not been commissioned.

## Every Sunday/Monday

1. Review next week's committed supply and paid limits. An approved fee cannot force an unready or irrelevant placement.
2. Reconfirm stock, staff, hours, QR and independent fallback. Update evidence rather than copying an old “ready” flag.
3. Open **Fulfillment → Review and publish a weekly release**. Select the week and counts by destination. Confirm that counts cover the entire admitted cohort exactly once.
4. Review the plan and publish. The transaction checks the full cohort, inventory, readiness, fallback, classification and approved commercial limits before writing grants. A retry of the same plan returns the existing release.
5. Inspect issued grants and Today's uncovered-member warning. Opening a member page never creates a new grant.
6. Check message preparation and dispatch separately. Only opted-in, unsuppressed members receive promotional messages after carrier/legal/environment gates are open. Everyone admitted retains web access to issued benefits.
7. Reconcile incidents, recoveries, credits, costs and labor for the week. A revised Program cannot rewrite already issued promises.

## After four weeks

1. Keep the original admitted denominator. Review first digital use, use in two or more weeks and week-four use only after that week finishes.
2. Treat 50% first use, 30% repeat and 25% week-four use as initial management targets. They are not scientific thresholds or evidence of incremental sales.
3. Review promise coverage, unresolved incidents and whether repairs actually reached members.
4. Review partner execution, source-attributed admissions and independently obtained evidence of off-site distribution. Do not call a screen play a view.
5. Ask the merchant for a real renew / repair / stop decision. Record it separately from free-benefit usage.
6. Reconcile negotiated Program fees once, credits, actual cash, expenses, benefit exposures, recovery reserves and retail value separately. Review active weekly operations against the initial three-hour target, excluding setup.
7. Agree the next run only after resolving the concrete constraints. Close the completed run without deleting its cohort or evidence.

## Physical rehearsal record — required at the real counter

Use designated internal testers and classification. An automated test or browser screenshot cannot certify a physical handoff.

| Step                                                     | Person records                                            |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Manager, address and useful opening hours checked        | Name, time, evidence                                      |
| Every serving shift understands the free item            | Staff names and briefing                                  |
| Primary stock and independent fallback counted           | SKU, units, dependency, payer                             |
| Program, weekly supply, run and partner source reviewed  | IDs and approved versions                                 |
| Adult tester joins with SMS unchecked                    | Membership active; no marketing consent                   |
| Same tester opens a backed weekly grant                  | Exact free terms, size, hours, expiry                     |
| Optional SMS tester receives a permitted test message    | Provider outcome; receipt observed separately             |
| Member claims; staff presents QR; member confirms        | Correct location; one digital redemption                  |
| Staff physically hands over correct item                 | Witness/member acknowledgement                            |
| QR repeated and wrong-store attempt checked              | No second or wrong-location redemption                    |
| Stockout/refusal before redemption rehearsed             | Incident; backed remedy without false redemption          |
| Failure after digital redemption rehearsed               | Original evidence retained; recovery issued/redeemed once |
| STOP and saved web/recovery-code access rehearsed        | Membership/grant remain; promotional queue suppressed     |
| HELP and ordinary message reach support                  | Named responder; documented resolution                    |
| Costs, partner execution, Today and next release checked | Separate ledger entries and dated sign-off                |

Record the result, release SHA, device/browser, destination and unresolved steps. **No real-counter rehearsal is claimed by the software build.**
