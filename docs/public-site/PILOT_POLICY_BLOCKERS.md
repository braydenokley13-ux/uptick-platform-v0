# Pilot policy blockers

Public pilot enrollment must remain closed until the items below are finalized and reflected consistently in the public and operating policy pages.

## Business identity and notices

- Confirm the operating legal entity name and business form. Do not substitute the Uptick Local brand name for the legal entity.
- Confirm the physical or mailing notice address that can be published in the Privacy Policy and Terms.
- Confirm whether another formal legal-notice contact is required in addition to the current support address, `iwhite@upticklocal.com`.

## Terms and privacy details

- Confirm governing law, venue, dispute process, liability language and state-specific disclosures with qualified review.
- Approve the retention schedule, deletion process, verified privacy-request process, response timelines, appeal route and authorized-agent process.
- Confirm the production incident and child-information response procedures. Pilot admission is currently limited to adults age 18 or older.

## Enrollment and messaging launch

- Approve the final enrollment language and admission workflow for a pilot targeting 150 adult members with a hard cap of 200.
- Confirm that each published weekly release is backed by approved inventory and location readiness before member benefits are issued.
- Complete and approve the production SMS sender, carrier registration, HELP response, STOP/START handling and consent-record workflow.
- Verify that web membership access works without SMS and that STOP preserves membership and already issued benefits.

## Publication gate

Do not enable the public membership join destination or production messaging until the operating identity, notice address, launch workflow and required review above are complete. The public site requires both a configured join URL and `NEXT_PUBLIC_UPTICK_PILOT_ENROLLMENT_OPEN=true` before it renders a join action.
